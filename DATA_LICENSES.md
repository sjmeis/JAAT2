## 3. Title Normalization and Auxiliary Data (`titles.parquet`, `SOC_map.json`)

* Compiled from public domain Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) mappings published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS).

## 4. AI Taxonomy (`ai.csv`)

The AI taxonomy data was created separately by the authors of this software package in a paper titled *Building a Custom Taxonomy of AI Skills and Tasks from the Ground Up with Job Postings* (https://aclanthology.org/2026.customnlp4u-1.11/).

The artifacts produced by this work were used for the AI taxonomy in this software package, distributed under a permissive MIT license.
